# NEM SEC evidence review

In Company Workbench → 研究记录, use **只更新财报**, then **保存当前 SEC 证据**. Save again to compare the same observation, or refresh explicitly before a later capture. The two selectors replay any saved pair. **下载当前证据包** and **导入证据包** provide portable JSON replay. Imported files must match the selected profile. No human tester is needed for this engineering workflow.

The first scope is NEM, CIK 0001164727, annual direct revenue, operating income, cash from operations and capital expenditures. A packet keeps normalized facts, concept, unit, start/end, accession, form, filing/publication day, original SEC observation, source URL, raw-response digest and the unchanged source-rights registry digest. Publication has date precision only. The HTTP receipt completion is used for observation time, rather than the earlier request-start timestamp. Missing direct facts retain the bounded upstream reason where supplied.

This is issuer financial evidence, not proof of common-stock identity, domicile, current listing status, price eligibility, peer comparability or point-in-time coverage. NEMCL is not substituted. No price, EPS, free cash flow or complete raw source response is added to a packet. Source rights remain those in `config/source_rights.yml`; current policy mismatch withholds comparisons. A checksum establishes internal consistency, not SEC authenticity. An imported file is user-supplied evidence and is not reauthenticated against the source.

Explicit captures are immutable generated JSON under ignored `outputs/local/sec-research-reviews/<profile digest>/NEM`. A repeat save records another capture time while retaining original evidence dates. Duplicate import is idempotent. Reading, replaying and comparing saved packets perform no transport or writes. The normal NEM route without `online=1` allows offline replay using the repository's registered company; Monitor returns to that route. An online identity search remains a separate source operation.

Four direct fields or explicit gaps are required. Imported content is limited to 64 KiB and history to 100 packets per profile. Unsupported schemas, duplicate keys/fields, nonfinite values, mismatched digests, unqualified identity, mixed within-packet durations and malformed provenance are rejected. Files are never overwritten. Unreadable or corrupt storage is shown as an error. Retention lifecycle beyond 100 captures remains a future feature; retain exported evidence before manually archiving files.

Comparison first checks current rights, profile, issuer and annual period, then each field's duration, concept and unit. Compatible value changes reuse existing research snapshot events but do not enter the process-event/journal store. Field order and capture timestamps do not create changes. Identical missing evidence is **unchanged with an open gap**, not complete coverage. Conflicting normalized facts under an identical raw digest are held. Monitor shows the latest two captures in actual UTC order, pending gaps and changed fields separately from process follow-ups.

Real-source verification on November 2 used NEM FY2025 10-K accession `0001164727-26-000010`, filed February 19, 2026. The observation supplies revenue, operating cash flow and capex; operating income remains withheld. Saving the same real observation twice proves unchanged replay, not an independently changed filing. Changed-value and incompatible-input behavior is tested with explicitly synthetic inputs. Real observations and screenshots stay private and ignored; code, synthetic tests and neutral documentation may be synced independently of data distribution.
