# NEM SEC evidence review

The [multi-company review contract](SEC_RESEARCH_REVIEWS.md) now reuses this workflow for qualified annual Companyfacts observations. This document retains the NEM v1/v2 and exact-filing method boundaries; NEM's unresolved field is not a gate on independent issuer qualification.

In Company Workbench → Research Notes, use **Financials only**, then **Save current SEC evidence**. Save again to compare the same observation, or refresh explicitly before a later capture. The two selectors replay any saved pair. **Download current evidence package** and **Import evidence package** provide portable JSON replay. Imported files must match the selected profile. Automated checks cover the engineering workflow; independent researcher validation remains a separate gate.

The first scope is NEM, CIK 0001164727, annual direct revenue, operating income, cash from operations and capital expenditures. A packet keeps normalized facts, concept, unit, start/end, accession, form, filing/publication day, original SEC observation, source URL, raw-response digest and the unchanged source-rights registry digest. Publication has date precision only. The HTTP receipt completion is used for observation time, rather than the earlier request-start timestamp. Missing direct facts retain the bounded upstream reason where supplied.

This is issuer financial evidence, not proof of common-stock identity, domicile, current listing status, price eligibility, peer comparability or point-in-time coverage. NEMCL is not substituted. No price, EPS, free cash flow or complete raw source response is added to a packet. Source rights remain those in `config/source_rights.yml`; current policy mismatch withholds comparisons. A checksum establishes internal consistency, not SEC authenticity. An imported file is user-supplied evidence and is not reauthenticated against the source.

Explicit captures are immutable generated JSON under ignored `outputs/local/sec-research-reviews/<profile digest>/NEM`. A repeat save records another capture time while retaining original evidence dates. Duplicate import is idempotent. Reading, replaying and comparing saved packets perform no transport or writes. The normal NEM route without `online=1` allows offline replay using the repository's registered company; Monitor returns to that route. An online identity search remains a separate source operation.

Four direct fields or explicit gaps are required. Imported content is limited to 64 KiB and history to 100 packets per profile. Unsupported schemas, duplicate keys/fields, nonfinite values, mismatched digests, unqualified identity, mixed within-packet durations and malformed provenance are rejected. Files are never overwritten. Unreadable or corrupt storage is shown as an error. Retention lifecycle beyond 100 captures remains a future feature; retain exported evidence before manually archiving files.

Comparison first checks current rights, profile, issuer and annual period, then each field's duration, concept and unit. Compatible value changes reuse existing research snapshot events but do not enter the process-event/journal store. Field order and capture timestamps do not create changes. Identical missing evidence is **unchanged with an open gap**, not complete coverage. Conflicting normalized facts under an identical raw digest are held. Monitor shows the latest two captures in actual UTC order, pending gaps and changed fields separately from process follow-ups.

Real-source verification on November 2 used NEM FY2025 10-K accession `0001164727-26-000010`, filed February 19, 2026. The observation supplies revenue, operating cash flow and capex; operating income remains withheld. Saving the same real observation twice proves unchanged replay, not an independently changed filing. Changed-value and incompatible-input behavior is tested with explicitly synthetic inputs. Real observations and screenshots stay private and ignored; code, synthetic tests and neutral documentation may be synced independently of data distribution.

The operating-income follow-up adds an explicit exact-filing check to the same workflow. Version-2 receipts retain only check metadata: exact document, accession, CIK, form, annual duration, taxonomy/concept/unit, publication/acceptance/observation dates, raw-response digest, rights status and withheld reason. Version-1 receipts remain readable. The existing exact-filing adapter must validate its map, submissions and document receipts before attachment; the issuer, accession, filing date, form, duration, taxonomy and currency must match the current Companyfacts revenue anchor. A failed refresh replaces the attempted check; a changed Companyfacts observation requires a fresh explicit check. Replay performs no transport. Imported checks remain user-supplied, checksum-validated assertions, not source authentication.

The November 2 source inspection found no Companyfacts OperatingIncomeLoss history and no matching direct standard operating-income fact in the exact FY2025 10-K. The [consolidated statement of operations](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1164727/000116472726000010/nem-20251231.htm) reports a pretax subtotal, and its segment and non-GAAP measures have different definitions. These are not mapped to operating income. The automated finding is bounded to the supported direct concepts and matching contexts; it does not prove that every custom concept or possible derivation has been reviewed. A missing direct fact, conflicting facts, unusable proof and an archive direct fact with unregistered rights remain distinct holds. Archive source rights remain personal-inspection-only; no archive monetary value, raw text or substitute metric is added to the portable packet.

Company Records and Monitor display the same exact source and open gap. New observation/capture timestamps alone remain unchanged. A changed exact-source digest or finding is a review-metadata change, separately identified from financial value changes. The generic unreviewed-source task is closed, and the operating-income evidence gap stays open. Any derived operating-income definition requires its own supported method and rights assessment. Broader company, financial and price coverage remains a separate program.

## Exact-pair review disposition (2026-11-06)

In the selected local research profile, select NEM in Monitor's saved SEC review queue. With fewer than two saved observations, it asks for a second explicit capture and does not offer a disposition. Once a pair is available, **Open this evidence pair** opens **Pinned SEC evidence comparison** in Company Workbench Research Notes. **Return to this review in Monitor** preserves that exact ordered pair as newer observations are saved. The comparison still reports value changes, source-metadata changes, unchanged inputs and open direct-field gaps under the existing rules. AMD and DUK use the same bounded workflow with separate issuer and receipt scopes. [Saved SEC company reviews](SEC_RESEARCH_REVIEWS.md)

Back in Monitor, choose **Reviewed** or **Still open**, enter **Review reason**, then press **Save review status**. A nonblank reason is required. The latest status and reason for that exact ordered pair persist across local sessions in an ignored sidecar under `outputs/local`; they are scoped to the selected research profile and are separate from the immutable receipt JSON. Reviewing a pair does not fill NEM's operating-income gap, authenticate an imported receipt, alter the thesis journal, promote readiness, or grant source rights. A missing or invalid pair is unavailable rather than redirected to a different comparison. The sidecar records a research-process disposition, not a SEC filing revision or market alert.

The [English fictional demo](../README.md#external-reviewer-start-here) illustrates the same question with fixed synthetic receipts `FICT-FY25-01` and `FICT-FY25-02`: **Review saved evidence** opens **Saved evidence comparison**, and **Record a disposition** accepts **Reviewed** or **Still open** plus **Reason**. Its note and disposition live in the browser tab and reset on reload. The [AMD filing case](https://yuzej21.github.io/Stock-Analysis/amd.html) is a separate dated, read-only SEC example; it does not save a decision.
