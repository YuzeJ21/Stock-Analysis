# Peer earnings context and price observation coverage

## Intent and decisions

Continue the authorized online company research workflow without requiring human testers. Help a reader interpret an annual P/E difference by showing the reported earnings change behind it, and remove the measured one-year observation-range limitation without relaxing issuer, filing, fiscal-window or split gates. The user has delegated execution and technical choices; the method below is the executor's decision.

Approaches considered: adding vendor TTM/forward ratios would require new definitions and source qualifications; repairing amendment gaps requires explicit amendment-scope evidence; reusing comparative EPS in the already matched annual filing and requesting bounded two-year price history uses the established evidence path. Choose the last approach now. Amendment gaps and fiscal differences remain visible.

## Contract

- Personal research only. No new source approval, credentials, paid dependency, recommendation, saved-model promotion or canonical/universe change. Reuse the existing linked worktree and developer branch. Authorized GitHub sync follows passing automated and desktop/phone checks; no PR, merge, deployment or publication.
- Add `annual_eps_context(facts, current)` as a pure bounded helper in `src/online_peer_earnings.py`. Its caller has independently validated the facts receipt, issuer and selected annual EPS. Never fetch or mutate inputs.
- Comparative EPS must have the same accession, form, publication date, `us-gaap:EarningsPerShareDiluted` concept and `USD/shares` unit as the selected current annual observation. Its end is exactly the day before the current period starts; duration is 350–380 days. Reject conflicting duplicate values/periods, wrong metadata, nonfinite values and alternate-unit observations. Identical duplicate rows are harmless. Never substitute another accession or latest vendor EPS.
- Output current/prior values and actual periods when independently valid. Compute `current / prior - 1` only for a positive prior EPS, with finite magnitude at most 1e6. Otherwise withhold the percentage with a stable reason. Missing comparative context does not remove an otherwise qualified P/E or change median membership. It is reported historical EPS context, not organic growth, a forecast, PEG, a quality score or proof of fair value.
- Attach context to qualified issuer observations and independently validated excluded annual EPS diagnostics. Retain accession, source URL, observation date and digest through the existing parent observation. Recompute context from inputs on each cached read.
- Extend the existing chart adapter with keyword `history_range='1y'`, accepting only `'1y'` and `'2y'`. Default standalone price inspection stays one year. Peer collectors explicitly request two years. Limit raw and validated bars to 400 for one year and 800 for two years. Bind the declared range to the exact source URL; legacy one-year receipts without a range field remain valid. Unsupported range, redirect or contradictory metadata fails closed before use. No automatic retry or hidden fallback.
- Two years is an observation request, not proof of complete split history. Still require the actual first completed bar on or before the annual end, no post-period split, matching same completed recent quote/bar, and all existing identity/rights guards. Expose actual observed first/last dates and range in source details. Empty/missing responses retain their gaps.
- In Valuation show a distinct earnings-context block for the subject and references, with EPS change, prior/current values, actual periods, missing reasons and source links. Sources uses the same data. Explain that growth and P/E differ in meaning and no growth-adjusted valuation is computed. Keep the headline, five-section flow, mobile disclosure controls and saved tools.
- Clarify the older internal design's fiscal-window wording to match existing code/public method: each reference is within 35 days of the subject; no numerical policy change.

## Verification and delivery

Synthetic unit/integration tests must prove happy paths, same-accession restated comparative values, missing/conflicting/nonpositive/unit/metadata/period gaps, no fallback, unchanged P/E membership, bounded range and exact URL, actual coverage and post-period split holds, cache revalidation and commercial guards. Use real online TXN/QCOM and AMD routes as dated observations, never full-universe proof. Capture desktop 1280x900 and phone 390x844 for valuation/source states, with no exceptions/overflow and usable controls. Run the guarded full suite sequentially at the committed final code, verify protected hashes, sync only the tested developer head, and deliver a private hash-verified evidence packet and held PR draft. Hosted CI is a separate result.
