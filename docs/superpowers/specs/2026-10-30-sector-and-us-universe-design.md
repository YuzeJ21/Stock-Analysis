# Sector financial support and U.S. listing coverage

## Intent and authorization
Continue online company research with bank, utility and foreign-issuer definitions, and move coverage from twelve examples to the entire available official U.S.-listing inventory. The user explicitly requested autonomous implementation, automated verification and GitHub synchronization. Reuse the clean existing isolated feature worktree; preserve main, unrelated work and canonical/source-rights data. No additional design or execution approval is needed within that authorization.

## Approach
Extend the existing registered direct US-GAAP field path for bank net-of-interest-expense revenue, regulated/unregulated utility revenue and tax-inclusive customer revenue, keeping exact labels. Add an independent IFRS native-unit inspection path rather than weakening the existing USD contracts. Assemble the complete observed inventory from dated Nasdaq Trader directory pairs and the SEC exchange/ticker maps, retain unmatched and unknown records, and inspect financial availability in bounded serialized issuer batches. Existing twelve-company sample remains a useful diagnostic.

Alternatives considered by the implementer: a paid consolidated feed conflicts with the zero-budget/source-rights constraints; treating the SEC ticker map as a complete company census loses securities/venues and confuses OTC with exchange listings; silently making all financial histories multi-currency would weaken established contracts. The selected additive approach keeps those boundaries inspectable.

## Financial contracts
Only exact-source direct fields covered by the existing SEC Companyfacts field registry may be displayed. Bank net revenue is labelled net of interest expense; never sum net interest and noninterest income or substitute pretax income for operating income. Utilities use aggregate supported tags, never summation of subsidiary/segment values. Foreign IFRS revenue, operating profit, operating cash flow and narrowly defined PP&E purchases retain their reporting currency and qualified concept. Prefer the single non-USD currency in the latest reported context to convenience USD translations; multiple plausible reporting currencies are withheld. Pin concept/unit across a history; do not infer FX conversion, ADS ratios, growth percentages, Q4, FCF or valuation. Annual, direct quarter and cumulative/interim periods remain separate. A 6-K is usable only with an exact valid duration; its filing date alone is not a quarter. Latest revisions, invalid competing contexts and newer unsupported periods cannot resurrect old available coverage. Retain accession/form/publication/observation/source/digest/rights. Validate cached results again at the display and coverage boundary.

## Population and execution contracts
Inventory is the union of all non-test rows from nasdaqlisted.txt/otherlisted.txt and exchange-labelled SEC records. Validate exact schemas, source URLs, raw-byte digests, dates and size bounds. Preserve source symbols and venues, explicit share-class aliases only, ambiguous CIK associations, ETF flags and unknown security types. Separate listing count, unique CIK count, requested/attempted/available/failed/unprobed counts, industry unknowns, domicile unknown, directory presence, analysis eligibility and point-in-time coverage. Never claim all U.S. companies or complete venue coverage merely because both directories parsed. List residual listing-venue gaps (including LTSE, Green Impact and dual listings) and unknown venue codes; OTC and null-exchange SEC rows remain separate residual populations.

A load action fetches inventory only; another explicit action checks the next issuer batch (maximum twenty); ordinary rerenders do no network work. Reuse one map per inventory and one evidence observation per CIK per run. Failed attempts remain in the denominator. Cumulative progress is session-only; refresh inventory resets it rather than combining different dated inventories. CLI can traverse the complete available issuer queue with --scan-all, printing reduced coverage without raw financial amounts. No bulk price retrieval. Commercial/non-Personal mode blocks activation and reuse. Nasdaq/SEC listing and SIC metadata are personal inspection sources, not an invented commercial license. No raw source files or generated reports enter Git.
