Keep this sample distinct from the legacy 12-company financial-only study and 15-company peer study. Failures, unattempted companies and exclusions stay in the denominator.

## Filing and reporting repairs

Bound raw submissions arrays at 100,000 rows and normalized financial/current reports at 2,000, within the existing 16 MiB source receipt limit. Validate aligned arrays, issuer, accession, official dates and exact SEC paths. Filter irrelevant forms before the smaller normalized bound. Recognize safe HTML/XML/text/PDF/paper metadata without claiming any document parser supports those formats. A current-report event date is not a financial reporting-period end: retain it independently of acceptance/filing dates, while annual and 10-Q financial chronology stays strict. Unsafe malformed records must remain held with explicit reasons; never recover an older annual version past a malformed newest annual filing.

For valid IFRS 6-K financial facts, qualify only the exact accession/form/filed source in the inventory plus independently validated fact duration, currency and period. Event reportDate does not create a fiscal quarter. Add explicit interim coverage, and preserve the latest event/review-trigger scope separately from the newest supported financial statement. Missing original sources or unresolved amendments keep the affected period held. Valid interim fields cannot acquire usable metrics merely because coverage lacks a state.

Native reporting history must not be replaced by older convenience-USD history. Preserve the chosen source taxonomy/currency and fact definitions. Support an additional standard revenue concept only when verified evidence establishes its separate definition; never stitch different concepts or currencies into growth. Financial-entity DCF, unsupported SEC entity type, common-share/ADS basis, tax allocations and exact source amendments remain explicit exclusions unless independently qualified.
