Do not approve implementation yet. If the owner wants to preserve the option,
approve only the name **Research Rehearsal Lab** and the research-process purpose,
then require a second decision after privacy, misuse, accessibility, and storage
review. The safest first form would be session-only, without model processing,
directional product language, amounts, allocations, transactions, or performance
measurement.

## Isolation Conditions That Are Not Optional

Any future approved design must keep rehearsal state structurally separate from
readiness, source rights, provenance, Discover eligibility or ordering, Company
Brief conclusions, Monitor priority, Data Health counts, Proof History, forecasts,
valuation, outcomes, backtesting, calibration, Public mode, Operator evidence,
telemetry, logs, screenshots, and share packages. Hypothetical text can never
fill a missing authoritative input or unlock analysis.

Live holdings, account imports, broker connections, order routing, alerts that
imply action, recurring jobs, auto-trading, and investment-performance claims
remain outside the product boundary.

## Required Review Evidence Before A Later Planning Decision

1. A named misuse/privacy reviewer tests recommendation laundering, sizing,
   performance, evidence contamination, historical rewriting, and private-data
   leakage.
2. A named human-accessibility reviewer tests the actual approved environment.
3. The owner records the exact data fields, storage posture, retention period,
   export behavior, deletion claim, backup wording, and model-processing policy.
4. The owner confirms that failures and unavailable states remain fail closed.
5. The owner separately authorizes writing an implementation plan.
