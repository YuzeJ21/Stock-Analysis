# Online filing research for ICICI Bank (IBN)

Open IBN from Discover and choose **Research company**. Workbench reuses the matched SEC ticker map and submissions receipts to inspect the latest eligible non-XBRL 20-F. The existing Indian GAAP parser checks consolidated profit and loss and cash flow statement rows. The issuer does not need a prepared financial CSV. Companyfacts returned HTTP 404 in the November 11, 2026 verification, and the annual filing was available independently. [Exact SEC Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1103838/000095010326010820/dp249803_20f.htm)

## What the exact filing supports

The July 20, 2026 filing covers the years ended March 31, 2026, 2025 and 2024. Each reported year supplies seven INR fields. These are interest earned, other income, gross total income, interest expended, profit attributable to the Group, operating cash flow and signed fixed-asset purchases. Overview shows the Group profit comparison using two columns of the same statement. Financial Trends and Data Sources preserve the dates, row definitions, signed cash amounts and links to the original statement anchors. The cover records the American Depositary Shares as filed. Current security identity and ADS earnings have separate requirements.

## Quarter and year-to-date results

The Form 6-K filed October 22, 2026 supplies the unaudited consolidated AS 25 results. Workbench shows the July 1 to September 30 direct quarter and the April 1 to September 30 fiscal year-to-date period in separate cards. Both periods show reported gross interest, other income, total income before interest expense, interest expended and net profit. Operating cash flow and signed fixed-asset purchases are available for six months only. The app leaves both cash fields withheld in the direct quarter. It uses the printed statement dates and converts crore amounts to INR. The SEC event report date remains visible separately. [Exact SEC Form 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1103838/000095010326015987/dp255170_6k.htm)

## Visible gaps

Gross total income retains its bank definition. Generic revenue, generic operating income, directly reported consolidated net interest income, free cash flow and ADS valuation remain withheld in this evidence scope. Operating-company FCFF is excluded for the bank. Standalone figures have their own reporting scope. Annual evidence remains available when an interim source cannot be verified.

Saved research retains normalized annual and interim facts and source receipts for historical replay from Monitor. The filing archive remains personal inspection only, with commercial use and redistribution held. Public repository files contain the implementation, constructed tests and method summary.
