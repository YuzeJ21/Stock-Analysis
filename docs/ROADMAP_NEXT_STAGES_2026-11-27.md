# Project review and next stages

Reviewed November 27, 2026.

## Product assessment

Stock Research Command Center is an advanced local research beta. Its useful
core journey is implemented: identify a company, explicitly obtain observations,
trace financial evidence, inspect a compatible comparison or its hold, save
notes and an immutable research context, and revisit it through Monitor.
It has meaningful authorization, provenance, storage and browser regression
coverage. These controls do not establish broad filing coverage, intended-host
operation, independent accessibility, user adoption or commercial readiness.

The roadmap's immediate priority is release reliability. A completed full run
at `ba954c417c9f01ac04fec2219fed1f47cd841deb` reported **53 failures,
10,522 passes and three skips**. The separate research journey passed all six
mandatory desktop/phone cases and its supporting checks. A passing journey
does not make a failing repository release acceptable.

substitute for these outcomes or establish demand.
