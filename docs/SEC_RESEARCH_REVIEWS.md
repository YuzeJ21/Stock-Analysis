# Saved SEC company reviews

Company Workbench Research Notes reuses the NEM receipt workflow for qualified annual Companyfacts observations. New v3 receipts support US-GAAP/USD direct fields in 10-K/10-K/A filings, with a qualified direct revenue anchor. The four fields remain revenue, operating income, operating cash flow and capex. A supported company may have missing fields; this is not a complete financial model or valuation.

The receipt retains normalized permitted facts and exact source metadata, including ticker, CIK, SEC association URL/observation/digest, Companyfacts URL/observation/digest, accession, filing date, duration, concept, unit, rights registry digest and withheld reasons. A v3 import must have the exact schema, matching issuer/source provenance and a valid checksum. Checksums validate internal consistency, not authenticity; imports are user-supplied and are not reauthenticated with SEC. No price/vendor payload, raw source response or archive monetary value is added. Current rights-policy mismatch withholds comparison.

NEM v1/v2 receipts remain compatible and unchanged. The [NEM exact-source check](NEM_SEC_REVIEW.md) remains explicitly scoped; its absence finding cannot be applied to another company. Different issuer, company, profile, period, unit or concept does not produce a financial-change conclusion. Source association metadata changes are separated from financial changes; observation/capture timestamps alone remain unchanged.

The existing store is profile-hashed, then symbol-scoped. Limits are 64 KiB per imported receipt, 100 receipts per company and 100 company directories per profile. Limits are visible errors, not silent truncation or automatic deletion. A damaged or symlinked company store is withheld independently while other valid company records remain visible. Malformed child names are reported as separate inventory errors without traversal or inclusion in company counts; they still consume the bounded directory capacity. Profile/ancestor integrity failures stop that profile's inventory. Empty directories do not count as saved observations but still consume the directory limit; deliberate archiving/retention tools remain future work.

Monitor reports only companies actually saved in the selected profile, separately counting field gaps, missing baselines, changed comparisons and unreadable records. These categories can overlap and must not be added into a market-coverage total. The selected company has a specific next action and return link. SEC data review is separate from existing research-process/journal counts and does not create an alert or recommendation.

NEM, AMD and DUK support an explicit decision on the exact ordered pair opened from Monitor. **Open this evidence pair** pins that issuer and its two saved observations. **Return to this review in Monitor** reopens the same pair, even if another observation has been saved. **Reviewed** and **Still open** both require a nonblank reason. Monitor keeps the latest disposition in an ignored local sidecar scoped to the research profile, issuer and pair. **View latest evidence pair** returns to the issuer's latest pair, which has its own review status. Other issuers remain in the saved evidence queue under the broader review contract. [NEM exact-source method](NEM_SEC_REVIEW.md)

Saved valid receipts allow an offline Company Workbench route even when no company CSV row exists. That route is labelled saved SEC evidence, keeps local model availability false and does not turn its old identity into a current listing assertion. Ordinary replay does not fetch a new identity or financial response. Explicit online refresh independently verifies the current issuer. Foreign-currency/IFRS/20-F/ADS methods, security identity, active status, domicile, price association and point-in-time coverage remain separately qualified paths.

Real browser evidence and receipt values remain private in the stage packet. Synthetic tests demonstrate value changes and mismatches; an actual new filing is required before claiming independently observed revision detection. Missing NEM operating income does not block independent qualification of other supported company receipts.
