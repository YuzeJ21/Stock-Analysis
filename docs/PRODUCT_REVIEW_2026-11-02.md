# Product and market review — November 2, 2026

## Decision

Position Stock Research Command Center as a workbench for repeated, evidence-bound company review. The proposed initial user is an experienced individual researcher revisiting a limited company list. This is a product hypothesis, not evidence of demand. The near-term outcome is understanding what changed, whether a comparison remains valid and what to verify next.

## What exists and what is incomplete

The product unifies Discover, a five-section Company Workbench, research records and Monitor. Online SEC inspection and price observations do not require a prepared company CSV. Reported annual diluted P/E has explicit identity, source, period, currency, price-date, split and industry-reference gates. Exact-source scopes expose special security evidence without automatically qualifying a financial denominator. Saved tools, canonical readiness and online observations remain separate.

The strongest engineering attribute is explaining admission and rejection at field/security level. The biggest product gaps are reliable acquisition and a coherent review loop across sessions. Online peer observations remain session-only. Existing saved-state snapshots, the six-company field study and saved HTML brief cover different scopes; their presence does not establish portable online observation history. Annual reported P/E is neither TTM nor forward P/E, and industry/SIC agreement is not reviewed economic comparability. Broad listing inventory is not all-company analytical coverage.

## Current market evidence

Official pages checked November 2, 2026; these are vendors' published capabilities, not independent quality tests.

| Product | Published capability | Product implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Koyfin](https://www.koyfin.com/features/) | Financial tables/charts, screening, watchlists, dashboards and price/valuation/news alerts. | A broad research dashboard already has strong alternatives. |
| [Fiscal.ai Terminal](https://fiscal.ai/products/terminal/) | Source-linked financials, estimates, transcripts, filings, IR content, news and ownership; advertises auditable fundamentals. | Source links and an auditability claim alone are insufficient differentiation. |
| [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/pro/) | Financial history, saved screeners, watchlists, alerts and downloads, with published free and paid plans. | Breadth and basic convenience face an established low-cost alternative. |

Inference: the more defensible opportunity is a reproducible review record of changes, comparability and unresolved evidence. Differentiation is unproven until users can complete that task and choose to repeat it.

## Advantages, costs and changes

| Advantage to preserve | Cost or weakness | Adjustment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explicit source identity, units, dates and withheld reasons | Detailed evidence can obscure the immediate answer | Keep result first, then a concise explanation and expandable proof. |
| Conservative security/financial eligibility | Company-specific filing contracts require maintenance | Fix shared acquisition/replay contracts before adding more exceptions. |
| One company page with existing research tools | Online results and saved review records have different lifetimes | Deliver one permitted replay/comparison handoff using existing record infrastructure. |
| Failure-inclusive sample reporting | Sample success can be mistaken for market coverage | Preserve declared denominators, pending/failure states and population boundaries. |

## Flow audit from this run

1. Open Company Workbench and select valuation: the company, update actions and five sections are visible. The financial/price update and peer update remain separate actions; the header occupies much of the first desktop viewport before the valuation answer.
2. Request peer evidence: the former generic listing failure gave little explanation of which prerequisite failed. The continuity stage adds original listing dates, a visible recovery policy and safe per-source diagnostics.
3. Inspect sources: the valuation and source sections must disclose the same acquisition record without a second fetch. Dated listing recovery must remain distinct from financial and price dates.

Screenshots and responsive measurements belong to the private exact-stage release packet. These observations do not establish independent usability, screen-reader compliance or hosted availability. No new visual redesign is warranted merely to repeat an earlier polish stage.

## Ordered outcomes

1. Shared online acquisition continuity, original dates and held financial failures: this stage.
2. Permitted SEC receipt replay and deterministic cross-session company comparison, then a distinct Monitor data-gap handoff. Reuse existing snapshot/journal contracts after checking scope; no unqualified vendor persistence.
3. Measured complete research cases across industries. Separate economic-peer rationale from classification matches; qualify ADS/IFRS/FX and successor annual bases independently before accepting them.
4. Independently defined additional valuation metrics with period/component evidence; company queryability is not analytical completeness.
5. Maintained or operated distribution only after source rights, reproducible delivery, usage and operating-cost evidence. Automated tests establish software behavior; they do not establish demand or grant data rights.
