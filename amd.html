Filing & calculations

Read AMD’s Form 10-Q on SEC EDGAR · Accession 0000002488-26-000191

  • The consolidated statements and Note 4 compare the three months ended Sep 26, 2026 with the three months ended Sep 27, 2025.
  • Revenue growth is current divided by prior revenue minus one. Segment share is segment revenue divided by total net revenue.
  • GAAP operating margin is operating income divided by net revenue. The Q3 FY2025 margin was 13.7%. The year-over-year margin change uses the unrounded ratios.
  • The revenue index divides each quarter's net revenue by directly reported Q4 FY2025 net revenue, then multiplies by 100. The Q4 figures come from the earnings release AMD furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K. The quarterly table links to each source.
